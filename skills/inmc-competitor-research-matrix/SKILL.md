# Build a source-backed competitor matrix

Use this workflow when asked for this output. Request the relevant inputs below if they are missing. Use the sample only if the user wants to practice. Respond in the user’s language.

## Inputs

Choose one buyer, one job and up to three alternatives. Use public product/pricing pages or public excerpts supplied with URLs and dates. For practice the prompt contains a fictional market.

## Method

Create a table with alternative, buyer/job fit, evidence for each requirement, listed price and billing terms, source and date, and unknowns. Use public first-party sources or supplied excerpts; do not sign in, contact anyone, or invent facts. When browsing is unavailable, identify exactly what the provided excerpts establish. Normalize pricing only when units and billing terms match or a transparent conversion is shown. Close with one specific gap hypothesis, its weakest assumption and one quick validation test. Do not treat vendor silence as proof of absence.

## Practice input

Fictional buyer: freelance designer sending international invoices. Pine lists PDF invoicing at USD 12/month, tax export unknown. Birch lists sales-tax export at USD 84/year, PDF invoicing confirmed. Reed lists PDF invoicing on a free tier, tax export unknown. Figures are synthetic.

## Verify

- Every factual cell has evidence, a date or a clear unknown label.
- Distinguish listed prices, billing cadence and any converted figures. Separate evidence from product hypotheses.

Reference: [U.S. Small Business Administration · Market research](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis).
